BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

A few useful
answers.

Which limestones do you manufacture?

Our range includes Charcoal Black limestone, Umbrian Limestone, Kurnool Grey limestone and Worn Grey Limestone. Explore each product page for its available options.

Can I request samples and current batch photographs?

Yes. Tell us the stone and finish you are considering, your project use and your delivery location. Our team will confirm sample availability and courier arrangements before dispatch.

What information should I send for a quotation?

Include the material, finish, dimensions, thickness, quantity, delivery destination and required timeframe. You can request quantities in square metres, square feet, pieces or containers.

Will every piece look exactly the same?

Natural stone has variation in shade and texture. Review current material photographs and agree a sample range and finish before placing your order.

Can buyers in the UK, Europe and USA request stone samples?

Yes. Tell us your preferred stone, finish and delivery destination so we can confirm sample availability and shipping arrangements. For a bulk quotation, include quantities, dimensions, thickness and your destination port or postcode. Read our international stone buying guide.

Is Charcoal Black also called Kadapa Black?

Yes. Charcoal Black is the name we use for our Kadapa Black limestone. Both names refer to the same limestone in the Divya Stones range. Specify your required finish, dimensions and quantity when requesting a sample or quotation.

Is Umbrian Limestone the same as Tandur Yellow?

Yes. Umbrian Limestone is the name we use for our Tandur Yellow limestone, also called yellow limestone. It is manufactured in India. Request current samples to confirm the colour variation and finish for your project.
