BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

A few useful
answers.

Which natural stones do you supply?

Our range includes Kadapa Black, Tandur Yellow, Kurnool Grey and Tandur Blue limestone, along with 13 granite and 18 slate and quartzite varieties. Explore each product page for its available options.

Can I request samples and current batch photographs?

Yes. Tell us the stone and finish you are considering, your project use and your delivery location. Our team will confirm sample availability and courier arrangements before dispatch.

What information should I send for a quotation?

Include the material, finish, dimensions, thickness, quantity, delivery destination and required timeframe. You can request quantities in square metres, square feet, pieces or containers.

Will every piece look exactly the same?

Natural stone has variation in shade and texture. Review current material photographs and agree a sample range and finish before placing your order.
