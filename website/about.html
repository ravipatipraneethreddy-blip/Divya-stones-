OUR STORY / BETHAMCHERLA, INDIA

Stone is our material.
Commitment is our craft.

Established in 2002, Divya Stones has spent more than 20 years supplying limestone and fulfilling customer requirements. From our base in Bethamcherla, India, we serve architectural projects and trade buyers, welcoming enquiries from importers, distributors and project teams in the UK, Europe and USA.

A MANUFACTURING HERITAGE

From Bethamcherla,
with purpose.

Spread across approximately eight acres, our facility is the largest stone manufacturing unit in the Bethamcherla area. Advanced machinery supports our operations, with a team of 50–75 people working across cutting, finishing, sorting and preparation for supply.

With monthly production capacity of up to 40+ containers, we work with importers, distributors, contractors and project buyers. We manufacture four limestone varieties: Charcoal Black, Umbrian Limestone, Kurnool Grey and Worn Grey.

We are committed to ethical labour practices and follow strict health and safety guidelines across our manufacturing operations. Respect for our people and a safe working environment are central to how we work.
