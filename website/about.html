OUR STORY / BETHAMCHERLA, INDIA

Stone is our material.
Commitment is our craft.

Established in 2002, Divya Stones brings the character of Indian natural stone to architectural projects and trade buyers.

A MANUFACTURING HERITAGE

From Bethamcherla,
with purpose.

Our limestone manufacturing operation is based in Bethamcherla, Andhra Pradesh. A team of 50–75 people works across cutting, finishing, sorting and preparation for supply.

With monthly production capacity of up to 40+ containers, we work with importers, distributors, contractors and project buyers. Our website brings our signature limestones together with a wider range of Indian granite and slate.
