BEFORE YOU ORDER

Worn Grey limestone:
your questions.

What is Worn Grey limestone?

Worn Grey is the Divya Stones name for a blue-grey Indian limestone. Explore the gallery for natural, honed, brushed and tumbled finishes, as well as paving, coping and decorative formats. Confirm the exact combination and current availability with your enquiry.

Can I use Worn Grey for patio pavers and pool coping?

Paving slabs, stepping stones and pool coping are shown in the collection. Share your project location, drainage, thickness and finish requirements so the team can review your specification. Slip and frost performance should be confirmed for the intended application.

Which sizes and thicknesses can I request?

The listed thicknesses are 20 mm and 25 mm, with custom sizes available by enquiry. Send dimensions, edge details and quantities. For US drawings in inches, agree the final metric production dimensions and tolerances in your quotation.

How do I request a price for the UK, Europe or USA?

Send your area in square metres or square feet, or a piece count, with dimensions, finish, thickness and delivery country and port or postcode. Ask for current samples and batch photographs. Sample shipping, packing and freight scope will be confirmed in the quotation.
