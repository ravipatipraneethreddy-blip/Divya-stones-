Kurnool Grey Limestone

Grey limestone tiles with a neutral palette

Kurnool Grey limestone offers a neutral colour for interior floors, wall surfaces and architectural details. Compare the natural and finished surfaces in the gallery to see how texture changes the appearance. “Grey limestone” and “gray limestone” describe the same colour category; Kurnool Grey is our named variety.

Honed and polished options are listed for this stone, with cut-to-size supply available. Share the intended use before choosing a finish. For exterior paving or wet areas, discuss the required surface texture and project specification instead of assuming an interior finish will suit every setting.

BEFORE YOU ORDER

Grey limestone:
your questions.

Which grey limestone does Divya Stones supply?

Our neutral grey limestone is Kurnool Grey. We also offer Tandur Blue limestone for buyers looking for a blue-grey palette. Each variety has its own product photographs and finish options.

Can I order grey limestone tiles in custom sizes?

Yes, cut-to-size supply is available. The listed thickness options are 15 mm and 20 mm. Provide the tile or slab dimensions, quantity, surface finish and intended use so we can confirm your specification.

Is Kurnool Grey limestone suitable for outdoor paving?

Tell us about the outdoor location, drainage and intended use before selecting a surface. The listed honed and polished finishes should not be treated as an automatic outdoor specification. Ask our team to review available finishes for your project.

How do I get samples and a grey limestone price?

Request current batch photographs and samples of your preferred finish. For pricing, include the required area or piece count, size, thickness and delivery destination. Sample availability and shipping arrangements will be confirmed by the team.
