SOURCING / BUYER GUIDE

How to Source Natural Stone from India

A practical guide to preparing an export enquiry

IN THIS GUIDE
  1. Start with the exact material
  2. Specify sizes, thickness and quantity
  3. Agree a representative sample range
  4. Share the full delivery destination
  5. Review the written quotation
  6. Plan inspection, packing and dispatch
Discuss your requirements ↗
01

Start with the exact material

Name the stone, finish and intended use. A request for “black stone” can describe several different products; “Kadapa Black limestone, tumbled and brushed, for outdoor paving” gives our team a much clearer starting point.

02

Specify sizes, thickness and quantity

List every size separately and include the thickness and edge treatment. State whether the quantity is in square metres, square feet, pieces or containers. Include a drawing for shaped coping, corners, steps or custom cuts.

03

Agree a representative sample range

Review current product photographs and discuss samples before confirming production. Natural stone varies between pieces and batches. Agree the colour range, surface finish and dimensional tolerances that your project requires.

04

Share the full delivery destination

Tell us the destination port or delivery address, together with your target timeframe. Cargo weight, packing dimensions, route and destination arrangements affect the transport plan. Ask for the scope and validity of any freight quotation to be stated clearly.

05

Review the written quotation

Before ordering, confirm the selected material, quantities, price units, packing, payment stages, production timeframe and delivery responsibilities in writing. Requirements for inspections and shipment documents should also be agreed for your destination.

06

Plan inspection, packing and dispatch

Agree how finished material will be checked and what packing photographs or inspection records you need. Confirm crate counts and final shipping details before dispatch. Keep the approved specification and shipment documents together for receipt of the order.
