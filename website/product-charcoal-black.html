BEFORE YOU ORDER

Black limestone:
your questions.

Is Charcoal Black a black limestone?

Yes. Charcoal Black is the black limestone in the Divya Stones collection. Its natural appearance can range from charcoal to deeper black depending on the piece, surface finish, lighting and whether the stone is wet or dry.

Can I order black limestone paving in custom sizes?

Cut-to-size supply is available. Send a size schedule, thickness, finish, edge detail and total area or piece count. We will confirm manufacturing options and availability in your quotation.

Are black limestone samples available?

Ask for current batch photographs and a sample in your preferred finish. Include your delivery address so our team can confirm sample availability and courier arrangements.

How much does black limestone paving cost?

We quote for your specification rather than a single standard price. The quantity, dimensions, finish, thickness, packing and delivery destination all affect the quotation. Include your destination port or postcode when requesting pricing.
