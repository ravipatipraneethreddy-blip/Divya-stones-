Which limestone products does Divya Stones manufacture?

Divya Stones manufactures four Indian limestones in Bethamcherla, Andhra Pradesh: Charcoal Black, also known as Kadapa Black; Umbrian Limestone, also known as Tandur Yellow; Kurnool Grey limestone; and Worn Grey, a blue-grey limestone. Each product page shows photographs, finish options and specifications.

Which stone formats and finishes can I enquire about?

Our limestone range includes paving slabs, floor tiles, cobbles, stepping stones, wall cladding, steps and pool coping. Explore natural, honed, brushed and tumbled finishes. Available combinations vary by stone and format; confirm your dimensions, thickness, finish and edge details with your enquiry.

How do I request product photos, samples and a quotation?

Send the stone name, finish, sizes, quantity and delivery destination. Our team will confirm current batch photographs, sample availability, pricing and shipment arrangements for your requirements.
