BEFORE YOU ORDER

Yellow limestone:
your questions.

Where does Umbrian Limestone come from?

Umbrian Limestone is the Divya Stones product name for a warm yellow limestone sourced in India. Its country of origin is India. Review current samples to confirm the sand and ochre colour range, selected finish and format.

Does every yellow limestone slab have the same colour?

No. Natural stone varies from piece to piece. Review a selection of current batch photographs and request samples to agree a suitable shade range for your project.

Which sizes and finishes can I request?

The listed options include natural, brushed and honed finishes, 20 mm and 25 mm thicknesses, and modular or custom sizes. The gallery also shows other treatments. Confirm the exact combination and availability in your enquiry.

Can I request Umbrian Limestone pricing for the UK, Europe or USA?

Yes. Send the paving area or piece count, dimensions, finish, thickness and delivery country, port or postcode. Use square metres or square feet and state the units clearly. Our team will confirm pricing, sample arrangements and the proposed shipment scope.
