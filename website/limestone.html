B2B EXPORT SPECIFICATIONS

Bethamcherla Quality — Container Consistent

Every limestone slab and tile is quarried, cut, and calibrated at our Bethamcherla processing facility in Kurnool District, ensuring consistent shade, uniform thickness, and ISPM-15 export crating for zero-damage ocean transit.

Consistent Shade Sorting

Each batch is hand-sorted and graded at source to eliminate shade variation across multiple containers on large-scale projects.

Custom Thickness & Finishes

Available in 18mm, 20mm, 25mm natural cleft, honed, or bush-hammered finishes to project specification.

Export-Ready Crating

Seaworthy ISPM-15 heat-treated wooden crates with foam protection ensure intact delivery to ports worldwide.
