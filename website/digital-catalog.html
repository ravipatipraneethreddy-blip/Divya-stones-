CHARCOAL BLACK

Also known as Kadapa Black limestone.

A highly dense, fine-grained natural stone famous for its rich, striking black tones. Charcoal Black brings an unparalleled level of modern sophistication to premium outdoor and architectural applications.

  • Finishes Natural, Honed, Polished, Brushed, Tumbled
  • Thickness 20mm, 25mm, Custom Calibration
  • Applications Patios, Pool Coping, Walkways

UMBRIAN LIMESTONE

Also known as Tandur Yellow or yellow limestone.

Umbrian Limestone is our warm yellow Indian limestone for courtyard paving, patio pavers and garden paths. Compare current samples, surface finishes and formats, then request a quotation for your project.

  • Finishes Natural, Brushed, Honed, Sandblasted
  • Applications Garden Paving, Courtyards, Driveways

KURNOOL GREY

Subtle, silver-grey elegance.

The architects' choice for contemporary, minimalist designs. Kurnool Grey delivers a clean, uniform finish perfect for modern indoor flooring and commercial cladding.

WORN GREY

Distinctive bluish-grey tones.

Uniting raw natural strength with unique color variations. An incredibly versatile stone built to withstand extreme climates while elevating luxury pool decks and feature walls.
